In this underwater coral reef scene, replace all existing kelp in place with golden kelp whose long golden fronds stretch upward so the whole underwater forest glows gold. Add five emperor angelfish (deep-blue body with yellow concentric stripes and a yellow tail) and five Moorish idols (broad yellow, white and black bands, a long white dorsal filament and an orange snout). All fish swim in open water, fully inside the camera frame, separated from each other, and never intersect the seabed or the coral.
